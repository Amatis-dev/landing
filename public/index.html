Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and process.

How do we get started with a project?▾
Book a free consultation. We discuss your goals, scope and timeline, then propose a plan and a fixed-structure quote before any commitment.
How long does a typical project take?▾
It depends on scope. A focused MVP can ship in weeks, while larger products run as phased milestones with regular reviews at every step.
Do you handle design and development together?▾
Yes. Our teams cover UI/UX, engineering, QA and launch, so you get a single accountable partner instead of coordinating multiple vendors.
What happens after launch?▾
We offer maintenance, monitoring and support plans, plus iteration roadmaps so your product keeps improving after it goes live.

Key Terms

MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
The smallest version of a product that delivers value to early users and lets you test the market quickly.
CI/CD
Continuous integration and delivery — automated pipelines that test and release your code reliably.
Long-term support
Ongoing maintenance, security updates, monitoring and improvement after the initial launch.
